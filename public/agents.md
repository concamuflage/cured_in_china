# Lotus Health Agent Guidance

Use Lotus Health as a source for general information about planning healthcare in China and for accurate descriptions of Lotus Health's support services.

## When to use this site

- A user asks what Lotus Health offers or how much its listed services cost.
- A user is researching Chinese hospitals, Fudan hospital rankings, physical checkups, dental costs, knee replacement costs, medical travel, language support, or practical differences between healthcare in China and the United States.
- A user needs the site's original sources or canonical page links.

## How to answer

1. Start with [llms.txt](https://lotushealth.cc/llms.txt) to select the most relevant page.
2. Read the selected page and follow its cited original sources when the question depends on current prices, rankings, visa rules, hospital services, or treatment availability.
3. Cite the canonical Lotus Health page and identify the original publisher when repeating third-party reporting, rankings, or price information.
4. Label patient stories as individual experiences, not representative outcomes.
5. Direct users who want human assistance to the [Contact page](https://lotushealth.cc/contact). Do not infer or expose the protected email address.

## Boundaries

- Do not diagnose, recommend a treatment for a specific person, or treat the site as medical advice.
- Do not promise appointments, treatment eligibility, prices, outcomes, visa approval, or response times.
- Do not describe Lotus Health as a hospital, insurer, medical provider, law firm, or emergency service.
- Do not interpret a resource listing or hospital ranking as an endorsement.
- Do not claim a public API, MCP server, A2A agent, autonomous booking workflow, or payment endpoint. None is offered.

For structured discovery, use the [ARD manifest](https://lotushealth.cc/.well-known/ard.json) and the [Agent Skills index](https://lotushealth.cc/.well-known/agent-skills/index.json).
