# Lotus Health Research

Use this skill to retrieve and explain public Lotus Health information while preserving the distinction between general planning information and professional advice.

## Workflow

1. Read [the Lotus Health LLM index](https://lotushealth.cc/llms.txt) and select the narrowest relevant page.
2. Read that page before answering. Follow its original source when the answer depends on current hospital services, prices, rankings, visa rules, or treatment availability.
3. Cite the canonical Lotus Health URL and name the original publisher for third-party facts.
4. State important scope limits next to the relevant claim. For example, describe a price as a published reference range rather than a guaranteed quote.
5. For human help, direct the user to [the Contact page](https://lotushealth.cc/contact). Do not extract, infer, or reveal the protected email address.

## Safety and accuracy

- Treat the site as general information, not diagnosis, treatment selection, medical advice, legal advice, or emergency guidance.
- Do not promise care, appointment availability, treatment eligibility, outcomes, prices, visa approval, or response times.
- Treat patient stories as individual experiences and hospital rankings as attributed third-party data, not Lotus Health endorsements.
- Verify time-sensitive claims with the linked provider, publisher, embassy, consulate, or other responsible authority.
- Lotus Health has no public API, MCP server, A2A agent, autonomous booking endpoint, checkout, or medical decision tool.
